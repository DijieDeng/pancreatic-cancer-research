**Data sources:** ClinicalTrials.gov, peer-reviewed journals, NIH/NCI, PanCAN, ASCO, Nature Medicine, Cancer Cell

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) remains one of the deadliest malignancies, but 2024–2025 saw unprecedented momentum across multiple therapeutic fronts. A total of **1,503 ongoing clinical trials** are registered on ClinicalTrials.gov for pancreatic cancer, spanning from early-phase dose-escalation studies to pivotal Phase 3 registrational trials. Key breakthroughs include the first Phase 3 survival benefit for Tumor Treating Fields (TTFields), promising mRNA neoantigen vaccine data, KRAS-targeted therapies entering late-stage trials, and novel combinatorial chemo-immunotherapy strategies.

## 3. Recent Breakthroughs & News (2024–2025)

### 3.1 Tumor Treating Fields (TTFields) — First Phase 3 OS Benefit

The **PANOVA-3 Phase 3 trial** (Novocure/Zai Lab) demonstrated that TTFields combined with gemcitabine + nab-paclitaxel significantly improved median overall survival in unresectable, locally advanced pancreatic adenocarcinoma versus chemotherapy alone. This is the **first treatment to show a statistically significant OS improvement** in this setting in a Phase 3 trial. TTFields also extended pain-free survival by 6.1 months. Regulatory filings are planned in the US, EU, and Japan.  
*(Presented at ASCO 2025; published in JCO)*

### 3.2 mRNA Neoantigen Vaccine — Autogene Cevumeran

A **3.2-year median follow-up** of the Phase 1 trial (Nature, 2025) showed that patients with vaccine-induced T cell responses had prolonged recurrence-free survival (median not reached vs. 13.4 months, P=0.007). The vaccine induced CD8+ T cell clones with an estimated lifespan of ~7.7 years, with 86% of clones persisting at 3 years. A global randomized trial (**IMCODE 003; NCT05968326**) is now ongoing.

### 3.3 KRAS-Targeted Therapies

- **Daraxonrasib (RMC-6236)** — Revolution Medicines' multi-selective RAS inhibitor completed enrollment in the **RASOLUTE 302 Phase 3 trial** for metastatic pancreatic cancer. Results expected in 2026. Available under expanded access based on preliminary survival/QoL benefit.
- **JAB-21822 + JAB-3312** — Allist Pharmaceuticals' KRAS G12C inhibitor + SHP2 inhibitor combination in Phase 1/2 (NCT05288205).
- **KST-6051** — Kestrel Therapeutics' novel KRAS-mutant targeted agent entering Phase 1 (NCT07458347).
- **iExoKrasG12D** — Engineered exosomes with KRAS G12D siRNA showed stable disease and CD8+ T cell recruitment in a Phase 1 trial (Nature Communications, 2025).

### 3.4 Neoadjuvant Chemotherapy Advances

A **randomized Phase 3 trial** (Cancer Cell, 2025; NCT03750669) showed that neoadjuvant nab-paclitaxel + gemcitabine followed by modified FOLFIRINOX improved event-free survival (15.3 vs. 10.9 months, HR 0.71, P=0.0136) and overall survival (35.4 vs. 27.2 months) in resectable pancreatic cancer, with comparable surgical outcomes to upfront surgery.

### 3.5 Elraglusib (GSK-3β Inhibitor) + Chemotherapy

A **Phase 2 randomized trial** (Nature Medicine, 2026) showed that elraglusib + gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel improved median OS by 2.9 months (10.1 vs. 7.2 months, HR 0.62, P=0.01) and 1-year survival (44.1% vs. 22.3%) in metastatic PDAC. A Phase 3 trial is being planned.

### 3.6 KRAS mRNA Vaccine — ELI-002 (AMPLIFY-201)

Final results of the **AMPLIFY-201 Phase 1 trial** (Nature Medicine, 2025) showed that the lymph node-targeted mKRAS amphiphile vaccine induced potent CD4+/CD8+ T cell responses. Patients above the immunogenicity threshold had median RFS not reached vs. 3.02 months (HR 0.12, P=0.0002). A randomized Phase 2 study (NCT05726864) with a 7-peptide formulation is ongoing.

### 3.7 Liposomal Irinotecan (HR070803) + mFOLFOX

A **Phase 1 trial** (BMC Medicine, 2025) demonstrated acceptable toxicity, good antitumor activity, and favorable pharmacokinetics for HR070803 + 5-FU/LV + oxaliplatin as first-line treatment for advanced/metastatic PDAC. A Phase 3 trial comparing this regimen to gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel is underway.

### 3.8 FDA Approvals & Regulatory Milestones

- **Cabozantinib (CABOMETYX)** — FDA approved for pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors (PNETs) after prior therapy progression (2025).
- **Zenocutuzumab** — FDA approved for lung and pancreatic cancers with NRG1 gene fusions.
- **NALIRIFOX** — Approved as a new first-line chemotherapy regimen for metastatic PDAC, showing superiority over gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel.
- **PT886** — Anti-Claudin 18.2 bispecific antibody fast-tracked by FDA for advanced/metastatic pancreatic cancers.

### 3.9 Emerging Modalities

- **CAR-NK cell therapy** (CL-NK-001, NCT06816823) — First-in-human CAR-NK trial in pancreatic cancer, ongoing in China.
- **TP53 R248Q TCR-T cell therapy** (NCT06619886) — Targeting TP53 hotspot mutations in solid tumors including pancreatic cancer.
- **EUS-guided radiofrequency ablation + immunotherapy** (NCT06831136) — Combining local tumor ablation with pembrolizumab.
- **Intratumoral MMR vaccine** (NCT07539155) — Novel intratumoral injection approach in borderline resectable/unresectable PDAC.
- **NanoKnife irreversible electroporation** (NCT06937996) — For unresectable pancreatic cancer.

## 4. Emerging Themes & Research Directions

1. **KRAS remains the #1 target** — With >90% of PDAC harboring KRAS mutations, multiple approaches (small molecules, vaccines, exosomes, CAR-T) are converging.
2. **Immunotherapy combinations** — Despite historically poor immunotherapy response in PDAC, novel strategies (vaccines, ablation + checkpoint inhibitors, CAR-NK) are showing early promise.
3. **Neoadjuvant strategy shift** — Growing evidence supports neoadjuvant chemotherapy before surgery, changing the treatment paradigm for resectable disease.
4. **Liquid biopsy & early detection** — Multiple ongoing trials (ORACLE/Guardant Reveal, LINFU registry) focus on ctDNA and circulating tumor cell detection.
5. **Spatial and functional imaging** — Intravital microscopy, novel PET tracers, and fluorescence imaging are advancing real-time tumor characterization.
6. **Microbiome & cachexia research** — Studies on digestive microbiota and cancer cachexia biomarkers highlight supportive care innovation.
