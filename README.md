## 🔬 Emerging Treatment Modalities (from Trial Sampling)

A review of the first 50 trials reveals several distinct treatment categories:

### 1. Immunotherapy & Vaccine Approaches
- **mRNA neoantigen vaccines** (e.g., autogene cevumeran) — personalized vaccines targeting patient-specific tumor mutations
- **CAR-NK cell therapy** (CL-NK-001) — first-in-human CAR-NK trial for pancreatic cancer
- **TCR-T cell therapy** targeting TP53 R248Q mutations
- **Intratumoral MMR vaccine** injection for borderline resectable/unresectable PDAC
- **Dendritic cell vaccines** (MesoPher) combined with CD40 agonist antibodies
- **iNKT cell + PD-1+CD8+ T cell** infusions
- **PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors** (pembrolizumab, sintilimab, dostarlimab) in combination regimens

### 2. Targeted Therapies
- **KRAS inhibitors** — KST-6051 (pan-KRAS), JAB-21822 (KRAS G12C) + JAB-3312 (SHP2 inhibitor), daraxonrasib (RMC-6236, multi-RAS)
- **PRMT5 inhibitor** (BAY 3713372) for MTAP-deleted tumors
- **PARP1 inhibitor** (HS-10502) for HRR-mutant/HRD-positive tumors
- **Claudin 18.2 ADC** (IBI343, TST001) — targeting CLDN18.2-positive tumors
- **HER3 monoclonal antibody** (HMBD-001) for HER3-positive tumors
- **HER2-targeted therapy** (zanidatamab) for HER2-positive tumors
- **Trop-2 ADC** (sacituzumab govitecan) + capecitabine
- **PD-L1 ADC** (PF-08046054/SGN-PDL1V)

### 3. Locoregional & Ablative Therapies
- **EUS-guided radiofrequency ablation (RFA)** — multiple trials combining RFA with chemotherapy and immunotherapy
- **Irreversible electroporation (IRE/NanoKnife)** for unresectable tumors
- **Hepatic artery infusion (HAI) chemotherapy** — FUDR and NASOX regimens for liver metastases
- **Stereotactic body radiotherapy (SBRT)** — preoperative and in combination with maintenance therapy
- **Tumor Treating Fields (TTFields)** — Novocure's PANOVA-3 Phase 3 showing OS benefit

### 4. Surgical Innovation
- **Laparoscopic vs. open pancreaticoduodenectomy** — multicenter RCT (TJDBPS07)
- **ICG fluorescence-guided** assessment of pancreatic remnant perfusion
- **Preoperative biliary drainage** impact on oncological outcomes

### 5. Novel Drug Combinations
- **mFOLFIRINOX + dostarlimab + vitamin D3** (DOVIPA trial)
- **Neoadjuvant chemoimmunotherapy** (NAI + sotevtamab + zabadinostat + gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel)
- **Anlotinib + benmelstobart + AG regimen** (ALTER-PA-001)
- **pH-modulated HAIC** (sodium bicarbonate + NASOX + PD-1 inhibitors)
- **Gemcitabine + leflunomide** repurposing approach
- **Namodenoson** (A3 adenosine receptor agonist)

### 6. Diagnostics & Biomarkers
- **ctDNA/MRD monitoring** (Guardant Reveal ORACLE study)
- **LINFU®** non-invasive pancreatic fluid cytology for early detection
- **68Ga-PFA2 PET imaging** targeting Annexin A2
- **MNPR-101-DFO*-89Zr PET** imaging for uPAR-expressing tumors
- **Liquid biopsy** (CTC/DTC/cfDNA) from blood, urine, and bone marrow
- **Pancreatic cyst registry** (UCSF PANC Cyst) for early cancer detection

## 📰 Recent News & Breakthroughs (2024–2025)

### 1. mRNA Neoantigen Vaccines Show Durable Immunity (Nature, Feb 2025)
- **Autogene cevumeran** (BioNTech/MSKCC) — individualized mRNA-lipoplex vaccine
- 3.2-year follow-up of Phase 1 trial: 8/16 patients mounted strong CD8+ T cell responses
- Responders had prolonged recurrence-free survival (median not reached) vs. 13.4 months in non-responders (p=0.007)
- Vaccine-induced T cell clones estimated to persist ~7.7 years; ~20% may survive decades
- Recurrent tumors showed pruning of vaccine-targeted clones (immune editing)
- Global randomized trial **IMCODE 003** (NCT05968326) ongoing

### 2. Off-the-Shelf KRAS Vaccine (ELI-002 2P) — Phase 1 (Nature Medicine, Aug 2025)
- Non-personalized vaccine targeting KRAS mutations (present in ~90% of PDAC)
- 85% of 25 participants mounted immune response; ~67% had robust responses
- Pancreatic cancer patients: median OS 29 months, recurrence-free >15 months post-vaccination
- Demonstrates feasibility of off-the-shelf KRAS-targeted vaccines

### 3. Daraxonrasib (RMC-6236) — Multi-RAS Inhibitor (NEJM, May 2026)
- Revolution Medicines' investigational oral RAS(ON) inhibitor
- FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation (June 2025); accelerated review pilot program (Oct 2025)
- Phase 1/2 results: median 8.5 months without disease worsening in pretreated metastatic PDAC
- **RASOLUTE 302** Phase 3 trial completed enrollment; results expected 2026
- FDA issued "safe to proceed" letter for expanded access (May 2026)

### 4. Tumor Treating Fields (TTFields) — PANOVA-3 Phase 3 (ASCO 2025)
- Novocure's TTFields + gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel vs. chemotherapy alone
- First Phase 3 trial to show statistically significant OS benefit in locally advanced PDAC
- ~2-month improvement in median overall survival
- Significant pain-free survival benefit (15.2 vs. 9.1 months, p=0.027)
- No additive systemic toxicity
- FDA submission planned for H2 2025

### 5. Neoadjuvant PAXG Regimen — CASSANDRA Trial (ASCO 2025)
- PAXG (cisplatin, nab-paclitaxel, capecitabine, gemcitabine) vs. mFOLFIRINOX
- Improved event-free survival: median 16 months vs. 10 months
- For resectable/borderline resectable PDAC in neoadjuvant setting

### 6. Dendritic Cell Vaccine + CD40 Agonist (Nature Communications, Nov 2025)
- **MesoPher** (autologous DC vaccine) + **mitazalimab** (CD40 agonist)
- Phase I trial in metastatic PDAC after mFOLFIRINOX
- Safe and well-tolerated; induced vaccine-specific T cell responses
- Increased intratumoral T cell infiltration; decreased collagen deposition
- 50% of patients showed stable disease

### 7. OSE2101 Cancer Vaccine + FOLFIRI (ASCO 2025)
- Therapeutic vaccine OSE2101 added to maintenance chemotherapy
- Statistically significant improvement in 1-year overall survival with minimal toxicity

### 8. Cabozantinib (CABOMETYX) FDA Approval (2025)
- Multi-target tyrosine kinase inhibitor approved for pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors (PNETs)
- For patients whose tumors progressed after prior therapy

## 🎯 Key Themes & Research Priorities

1. **Immunotherapy awakening** — Multiple vaccine platforms (mRNA, dendritic cell, peptide, off-the-shelf KRAS) are showing durable T cell responses in PDAC, a historically immunotherapy-resistant cancer
2. **KRAS druggability** — After decades of being "undruggable," RAS mutations (present in >90% of PDAC) are now targetable with both inhibitors (daraxonrasib) and vaccines (ELI-002)
3. **Locoregional innovation** — EUS-RFA, IRE, TTFields, and HAIC are expanding the therapeutic toolkit for locally advanced disease
4. **Precision medicine** — Biomarker-driven trials targeting MTAP deletion, CLDN18.2, HER2, HER3, Trop-2, BRCA/HRR mutations, and BRAF fusions
5. **Combination strategies** — Nearly all novel agents are being tested in combination with backbone chemotherapy (mFOLFIRINOX, gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel)
6. **Early detection** — Liquid biopsy, pancreatic cyst registries, and novel imaging agents aim to shift diagnosis earlier
